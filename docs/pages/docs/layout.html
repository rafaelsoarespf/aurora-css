Column Span

The grid is based on 12 columns. Use .col-1 through .col-12 to give an element a fixed width as a fraction of 12. Children without a .col-* class fill the remaining space equally between them.

Note: .col-N is always a fixed fraction of 12 columns (e.g. .col-2 is always 16.66%, regardless of how many siblings share the row) — it does not divide relative to other .col-N siblings. Elements without a .col-* class automatically share whatever space is left.
